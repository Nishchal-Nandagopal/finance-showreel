How to read this

Every number on this page is compiled from each project's results file or a cached public series. None is typed in by hand. Each desk names its evidence files.

What it isn't

  • Not investment advice. Personal projects on public data; no positions held.
  • The clients in P4 and P5 are fictional. The data is not.
  • The trading book is a dry run until the live 12 weeks exist.

Known limits

  • Free price data covers surviving companies, so the quant and harvesting studies carry survivorship bias.
  • LedgerLens' 50/50 is in-sample; the first pass scored 39/50.
  • The satellite NO₂ nowcast and the PE sourcing screen are reported nulls.
